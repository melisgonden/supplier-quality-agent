Supplier Quality Agent

Closes the quality loop for a print marketplace: reads customer complaints, finds who really caused each problem, puts the cost where it belongs, and opens corrective actions (CAPA) with suppliers that keep repeating errors.

Only complaints caused by the supplier count against them. Rules: Watch above 0.25 % defect rate; CAPA when the same defect repeats 3+ times.

Corrective actions

Opened automatically when a supplier repeats the same defect. Each CAPA gets an owner, a priority and a due date, and every change is kept in its history. The agent drafts the letter; a person sends it.

The problem

A print marketplace does not print itself: hundreds of partner printers and carriers do. Customer service resolves each complaint with a reprint or refund, but the loop often stops there. The real cause is not recorded, the cost is not charged to whoever caused it, and the same supplier error reaches the next customer.

What the agent does

  1. Reads complaints from the existing ticket stream, in any language. Nobody types anything extra.
  2. Classifies the defect and the root-cause owner, using the order data (which printer, which carrier, what the file check found).
  3. Assigns the cost to the right party: customer file, supplier or carrier.
  4. Finds patterns per supplier and product and opens a CAPA when a defect repeats.
  5. Drafts the CAPA request with evidence and suggests rerouting work until it is closed.

Who does what

AI reads and interprets messy multilingual text and judges the root cause. Code calculates rates, costs and thresholds, where AI should not be trusted. A person decides: every owner can be confirmed or overridden, and nothing is sent to a supplier automatically.

How I would measure it

Recovered cost per month, repeat-defect rate per supplier after a CAPA, CAPA closing time, and how often the quality specialist overrides the agent (the agent's own quality KPI).

Privacy: what the AI sees

Company names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, postcodes and IBANs are masked before anything is sent to the AI. The real names are filled in again only after the answer comes back.
